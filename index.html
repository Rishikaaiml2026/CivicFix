How CivicFix Works

A seamless, transparent loop connecting citizens with municipal field crews.

1. Snap & Report

Capture a photo, dictate using voice, and pin the exact GPS coordinates on our interactive map.

2. Smart Duplicate AI

Our proximity and text similarity engine flags matching issues nearby so you can upvote instead of duplicate.

3. Direct Routing

Automatically assigned to municipal departments (Roads, Sanitation, Lighting, Water) with SLA monitoring.

4. Verified Resolution

Staff upload photographic proof and notes before marking resolved. Citizens track live timeline updates.
